02 · The product

One cost model. Every screen.

Build the rota. Show the GM the consequence of every shift, before service and during it. Post the accrual to the ledger. The same numbers at every step, so the rota reconciles to payroll.

Each day's shifts laid over its sales curve, with the hours short or over beneath and the day's labour % beside it. Drag a shift to move it; the rota P&L on the right updates as you go.

Demand-led build/01

Built from the forecast, not from last week.

Each site's forecast comes from its own till history, weather, events, bookings and school holidays. Hourly sales and covers become headcount per role, and the build fills peaks first with the cheapest legal person.

  • Sales per labour hour or covers per person
  • Areas within a site: front, back, bakery
  • Unfillable gaps flagged as a hiring signal
True cost per shift/02

Costed the way payroll will cost it.

Gross pay, premiums, holiday accrual, employer NI, pension and paid travel between sites, worked out on the whole week and spread back over each shift. No more rate times hours.

  • Marginal cost of every shift, live as you drag
  • Sunday, bank holiday, night and overtime premiums
  • Live labour % and pound variance to target
Rota to ledger/03

The rota is the first document in the close.

Approved hours go to TeamPay per PAYE scheme. The labour accrual posts straight into each company's Xero, by role nominal and site tracking, with its reversal on the Monday after.

  • Accrual posted before payroll runs
  • Every pay run reconciled to the accrual
  • Built for a 3-day close

05 · During service

Who can go home, and what it saves.

The cut call reads the till so far against forecast, reforecasts the rest of the night at that pace, and turns it back into headcount. Each person not needed is offered as an early finish, dearest saving first, with the saving worked on their whole week.

Illustrative. Savings include holiday, employer NI and pension on each person's week.

  • One tap to apply. The shift stays published, the person is told on their phone at once, and the cut is logged.
  • Legal by default. Once short notice pay under the Employment Rights Act starts, what a cut owes comes off its saving. Before then it is shown.
  • Nobody has to be watching. While a site trades, whoever publishes its rota is emailed if trading falls behind with £25 or more to save, or runs 15% ahead with hours short.
  • Overnight trading counts. A bar open until 03:00 still reads that evening's trading at 00:20.
  • The labour flash beside it. Labour so far from the clock against takings so far, labour by close and headroom against target, refreshed every two minutes.

08 · UK compliance

Legal by default. Not by luck.

Every shift is checked as it is built, dragged, swapped or claimed, by the GM, the staff app, the AI or the API. Warnings get a second look. Blocking issues stop the rota being published until they are fixed.

  • WTR
    Breaks and daily rest20 minute break over six hours, 11 hours between shifts across week boundaries. Real exceptions are overridden with a written reason and the compensatory rest given.
  • WTR
    Weekly rest and the 48 hour averageA clear day off enforced, and a warning before a week takes someone's rolling 17 week average over 48 hours without an opt-out.
  • Night work
    Night workersWho counts as one worked from clocked time and the rota, the 8 hours in 24 average over 17 weeks, and the free health assessment offer recorded.
  • Young workers
    Under-18 and child employment8 hours a day, 40 a week, 12 hours rest, never between midnight and 04:00. School-age staff held to term-time and holiday limits by nation.
  • NMW / NLW
    Minimum wage by age, on hours workedEvery rate checked against the band for the person's age on the shift date, and pay tested over each pay period on clocked hours.
  • ERA 2025
    Employment Rights ActShort notice pay logged when a published shift is cut or moved inside the window, and guaranteed hours offers worked out with the letter. Start dates are yours to set.
  • Right to work
    Right to work and student hoursNobody without a valid check, a copy and a date can be rostered. Student visa holders over their term-time hours are flagged and priced.
  • Travel
    Moving between sitesA same-day move that cannot be made in time is blocked, using TfL journey times. Travel between sites is paid as working time.
  • Rostering
    Double booking, leave, absence and availabilityNo one rostered twice, on holiday, off sick or outside the hours they said they can work, at any site in the group.
  • Training
    Right person, right roleOnly people trained for a role can be put on it, with food hygiene, allergens, Challenge 25 and personal licence expiry tracked.
  • Tips Act
    Tronc and the Allocation of Tips Act 2023Tips kept out of takings, shared by the scheme's rules, paid by the legal deadline and flagged when they are late.
  • UK GDPR
    Staff data handled properlyRequest log with deadlines, one-click subject access export, erasure for leavers, and a retention schedule that statutory floors cannot undercut.

14 · Questions

The sensible questions.

How is this different from Fourth, Sona or Workforce.com?

Scheduling is a commodity. The difference is that our cost on the rota is the cost on the P&L: fully loaded, reconciled to payroll, and posted to the ledger as the labour accrual. The same model runs the cut call during service and the 13 week cash forecast for finance. The rota becomes part of the close, not a separate tool that finance has to reconcile after the fact.

Where does the forecast come from?

From each site's own hourly till sales: the same weekday and hour over the last eight weeks, recent weeks weighted most, with bank holidays, the UK trading calendar, weather, local events, school holidays and covers on the books allowed for. Square connects directly; other tills import from their CSV export. Accuracy is measured every week, and people's overrides are scored against the model.

Which accounting and payroll systems does it work with?

Payroll runs natively with TeamPay, one employer per PAYE scheme, with starters, leavers and approved hours synced and each pay run reconciled back to the accrual. The labour accrual posts straight into Xero by its API, one journal per company, or downloads in Xero manual journal format.

Do my staff need to download an app?

No. They open an invite link on their own phone, choose a password and add it to their home screen. It clocks them in, shows their rota, takes requests, carries messages and notifications, and shows their tips. Shared iPads at the door work as clocking kiosks.

We do not need all of it. Can we switch parts off?

Yes. An owner switches sections on and off from the Features screen. Nothing is deleted and nobody's access changes; switching a feature back on restores it as it was. Most groups start with the rota, clock-in and payroll, and add the cut call, coaching and prime cost as each habit takes hold.

Is the AI safe to let near the rota?

It works through the same compliance engine, cost model and access rules as a manager, so it cannot roster illegally or see personnel data it should not. Every change is shown for a person to confirm first. It runs on your own Claude key with spending limits you set, and an owner can switch AI rota writing off altogether.

Why per site and not per member of staff?

Hospitality hires for the summer, for Christmas and for every new opening, and turns staff over fast. A price per head charges you for exactly the flexibility a rota exists to manage. Per site, your bill follows your estate, not your headcount.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Subscribe at any point in the trial and you are not charged until it ends. If you do not, changes pause and everything stays readable, so your staff records and pay history are never held back. Subscribing picks up exactly where you left off.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from Billing at any time. Monthly plans run to the end of the month you have paid for; yearly plans to the end of the year. After that everything stays readable, so your records are never held back.

Who keeps the statutory rates and new law up to date?

We do. Minimum wage bands, employer NI, the pension band and holiday accrual are held in one place, set for 2026/27 and re-checked against GOV.UK before every new tax year. The Employment Rights Act 2025 measures are built in, with the figures the regulations will set held as settings until they are made.

What is coming next?

Direct connections to more tills beyond Square, to applicant tracking so new starters arrive with their details, and to digital right to work checks, plus payslips and P60s in the staff app as soon as TeamPay shares them. New features ship every week and are shown in the app each Monday.
