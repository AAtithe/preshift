05 · UK compliance

Legal by default. Not by luck.

Every shift is checked as it is built. Warnings get a second look. Blocking issues stop the rota being published until they are fixed.

  • WTR
    Breaks and daily rest20 minute break over six hours, 11 hours between shifts, checked across week boundaries.
  • WTR
    Weekly rest and the 48 hour averageWeekly rest enforced, 48 hours flagged for anyone without a signed opt-out.
  • Young workers
    Under-18 limits8 hours a day, 40 a week, 30 minute break, 12 hours rest and restricted night work.
  • NMW / NLW
    Minimum wage by ageEvery rate checked against the band for the person's age on the shift date.
  • Rostering
    Double booking, leave and availabilityNo one rostered twice, on holiday or outside the hours they said they can work.
  • Training
    Right person, right roleOnly people trained for a role can be put on it, across every site in the group.

09 · Questions

The sensible questions.

How is this different from Fourth, Sona or Workforce.com?

Scheduling is a commodity. The difference is that our cost on the rota is the cost on the P&L: fully loaded, reconciled to payroll, and posted to the ledger as the labour accrual. The rota becomes part of the close, not a separate tool that finance has to reconcile after the fact.

Where does the forecast come from?

From Preshift's own sales and covers feeds, by hour and by site. Forecast accuracy improves with every week of actuals. You set the labour target and the productivity rule for each role.

Which accounting and payroll systems does it work with?

The labour accrual exports in Xero manual journal format, by role nominal and site tracking category, with an hours file for payroll. Further payroll exports are on the roadmap for the payrolls WS clients run.

Who keeps the statutory rates up to date?

We do. Minimum wage bands, employer NI, the pension band and holiday accrual are held in one place, set for 2026/27 and re-checked against GOV.UK before every new tax year.

What is coming next?

A staff app for rotas, swaps, availability and leave; geofenced clock-in so actual hours replace rostered hours in the accrual; a live sales feed so labour % updates through the day; and tronc allocation under the Employment (Allocation of Tips) Act 2023.
