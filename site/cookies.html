Cookies and Similar Technologies

Version 2026-11-04, effective 4 November 2026

Preshift Rota uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Everything below is needed to sign you in, or remembers a choice you made.

Cookies

NameWherePurposeLasts
ps_sessionManager web app and demoKeeps you signed in12 hours (demo: until midnight)
ps_staffStaff appKeeps you signed in on your phone30 days
ps_kioskClock-in kioskLinks a shared tablet to its site365 days

All are first party, HttpOnly (scripts cannot read them) and SameSite=Lax. The marketing website sets no cookies.

Stored on your device

The manager app remembers display choices you make, such as your preferred rota view, screen density, which hints and panels you have folded away, and whether you have seen this week's What's new. These never leave your device. Item names begin preshift..

The staff app uses a service worker so it can receive notifications you have allowed, and so it opens quickly.

Analytics

The manager web app uses Vercel Web Analytics to count which screens are used. It sets no cookies, records the screen name only (never the site, week, any name or any token), and does not run in the staff app, the kiosk or the marketing website.

You can stop your visits being counted on this device and browser with the button below. It is remembered on this device until you clear your browser's data for this site.
