Data Processing Agreement

Version 2026-11-04, effective 4 November 2026

1. Parties and roles

1.1 This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is between the Customer (controller) and TeamPay Payroll Limited (company number 17197586, trading as Preshift Rota) (processor), and forms part of the Agreement defined in the Terms of Service. Terms defined there have the same meaning here.

1.2 Where a Customer group has several employing companies, the Customer enters into this DPA for itself and as agent for each employing company in its organisation, each of which is a controller of its own staff's data.

1.3 "Data Protection Law" means the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, each as amended. "Personal Data", "Special Category Data", "Data Subject", "Processing", "Controller", "Processor" and "Personal Data Breach" have the meanings in Data Protection Law.

2. Processing

2.1 We will process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including as to international transfers, unless the law requires otherwise (in which case we will tell the Customer first, unless the law prohibits it). The Agreement, the Customer's configuration of the Service, and its users' actions in the Service are the Customer's complete instructions. We will tell the Customer immediately if we think an instruction breaks Data Protection Law.

2.2 The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing, the types of Personal Data and categories of Data Subject are in Annex 1.

2.3 The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis, and where needed a condition under Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018 and an appropriate policy document, for all Personal Data it puts into the Service, and for giving Data Subjects the information Articles 13 and 14 require.

3. Our people

We will ensure everyone we authorise to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality, is trained in data protection, and has access only to what their role needs. Access by our staff to Customer Personal Data for support is logged.

4. Security

We will implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures in Annex 2, appropriate to the risk, as Article 32 requires. We may update them provided the overall level of protection is not reduced.

5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Customer gives general authorisation for us to use the sub-processors listed at our sub-processors page (Annex 3).

5.2 We will give at least 30 days' notice of any new or replacement sub-processor by email to the account owner and on the list page. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If we cannot reasonably accommodate the objection, the Customer may terminate the affected part of the Service and receive a pro rata refund of prepaid Fees.

5.3 We will impose data protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective than this DPA, and remain liable to the Customer for each sub-processor's performance.

5.4 Providers the Customer contracts with directly and connects using its own account or key (including its own Claude API account, email sending account, WhatsApp Business account, Twilio account and EPOS) are the Customer's processors, not our sub-processors.

5.5 Where the Customer also uses our TeamPay payroll service, we process payroll data under that service's own data processing terms. Data passed from Preshift Rota to the TeamPay payroll service is passed on the Customer's instruction and used only for running the Customer's payroll; we keep the two services' purposes, access and records separate.

6. International transfers

We will not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the UK except to a country covered by UK adequacy regulations, or under the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, supported by a transfer risk assessment. The mechanism for each sub-processor is in Annex 3.

7. Assisting the Customer

7.1 Data Subject requests. The Service provides tools for the Customer to respond to requests under Articles 15 to 21 (export, correction, erasure, restriction, and a request log). If we receive a request directly, we will pass it to the Customer within 3 Business Days and not respond ourselves except to acknowledge it.

7.2 Other assistance. Taking into account the nature of processing and information available to us, we will provide reasonable assistance with the Customer's obligations under Articles 32 to 36 (security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation). We may charge at our then current rates for assistance beyond providing standard documentation.

8. Personal Data Breaches

8.1 We will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

8.2 The notice will describe, as far as then known: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point. We will supplement it as more becomes known.

8.3 We will take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach, and will not inform third parties (other than law enforcement or a regulator where required) without the Customer's consent. The Customer is responsible for notifying the ICO within 72 hours and Data Subjects where required.

9. Deletion and return

9.1 On termination, the Service stays available read-only for 30 days so the Customer can export Customer Personal Data. We then delete it within 60 days, and backups are overwritten within 35 days after that, unless the law requires us to keep it.

9.2 During the Agreement, the Service lets the Customer set retention periods in Privacy and applies them when the Customer's administrator applies them.

10. Audit

10.1 We will make available all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28, including this DPA, Annex 2 and the sub-processor list.

10.2 Where that information is not reasonably sufficient, or a regulator requires it, the Customer (or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality) may audit our compliance on at least 30 days' notice, no more than once in any 12 months, during business hours, at the Customer's cost, and without access to other customers' data.

11. Liability and precedence

11.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in clause 13 of the Terms of Service.

11.2 If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of Service on the processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails.

Annex 1: Details of processing
Subject matterProvision of Preshift Rota workforce planning software
DurationThe Agreement, plus the deletion period in clause 9
NatureCollection, storage, organisation, retrieval, calculation, display, transmission to Connected Systems on the Customer's instruction, and deletion
PurposeRota planning and publishing; time and attendance; leave and absence management; working time, minimum wage and right to work checks; HR records; staff messaging; labour costing, forecasting, payroll exports and finance journals; wellbeing and engagement features the Customer enables
Data SubjectsCustomer's employees, workers and agency staff (including workers under 18 and school-age children where the Customer employs them); job applicants and new starters; the Customer's managers and administrators; emergency contacts of staff

Personal Data (ordinary): name; date of birth and age; email; mobile number; address and postcode; NI number; emergency contact name, relationship and phone; job roles, sites and line manager; employment dates, contract type, contracted hours, 48-hour opt-out, probation and notice; pay rate or salary, premiums, deductions, pension enrolment, tips and tronc shares; availability, leave, holiday entitlement and balances; shifts, timesheets and clock-in and out times and method; clock-in card, tag and PIN identifiers (PINs hashed); location at clock-in and out where the Customer uses location clocking; rota notes; messages, read receipts, channel preference and consent records; push notification subscriptions; written statement and handbook signatures (typed name, time, IP address, device); training and reviews; lateness, no-show, fairness, absence (Bradford) and retention risk scores; recognitions and staff meals; login and audit data.

Right to work: check date, evidence type, expiry, visa work limits, and copies of identity and immigration documents, held encrypted with the safeguards applied to Special Category Data.

Special Category Data: health data in sickness absence records, absence reasons, fit notes, return to work notes, disability and pregnancy indicators used to exclude absences from triggers, and night work health assessment offers.

Criminal offence data: none. The Customer must not store criminal records information in HR notes or documents.

Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or above) and at rest (database provider encryption).
  • Application level AES-256-GCM encryption of right to work documents, fit notes and HR files, with the key held outside the database; every view logged.
  • Passwords hashed with scrypt; authenticator secrets encrypted; kiosk PINs hashed; invitation and reset tokens stored only as hashes.
  • Two-factor authentication required for pay, payroll, journal, settings and access roles.
  • Role and site based access control, enforced on the server for every request.
  • Audit log of changes and of document views.
  • Tenant isolation: each organisation's data scoped by organisation on every query; automated tests covering access boundaries.
  • Rate limiting on sign-in, sign-up and demo.
  • Data minimisation in AI features: NI numbers, addresses, absence reasons, HR notes, bank and medical details excluded from data sent to AI models.
  • Backups by the database provider, with point in time recovery.
  • Hosting and database region: United Kingdom (London). Customer Data at rest is stored only in the UK.
  • Staff confidentiality obligations, data protection training on joining and annually.
  • Personal Data Breach handling and notification as set out in clause 8.
