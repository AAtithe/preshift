7. Your rights

You have the right to: be informed; access your data; have it corrected; have it erased; restrict its use; data portability; object to processing based on legitimate interests (including analytics and product emails); and not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects without the safeguards the law requires.

To use any right, email hello@preshift.co.uk. We will reply within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you why). We may need to confirm your identity. There is normally no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, tell us first and we will investigate and reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Sensitive documents (right to work copies, fit notes, HR files) are encrypted with a separate key held outside the database, and every view is logged. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Two-factor authentication is required for access to pay, payroll, journals, settings and access control. Access by our own staff is limited to what support needs and is logged.

9. If you are a member of staff at a business using Preshift

Your employer is the controller. Depending on what your employer switches on, Preshift may hold:

  • About you and your work: name, date of birth, contact details, roles, pay rate or salary, contracted hours, start date, availability, leave and holiday, rota notes.
  • Clocking: when and how you clocked in and out (card, kiosk PIN, phone tag or app). If your employer uses location clocking, your phone's position at the moment of clocking only, kept for 3 months unless your employer sets a different period.
  • HR records: NI number, address, emergency contact, contract and handbook signatures (including the IP address and device used to sign), training, reviews, and any formal cases.
  • Right to work: check date, document type and expiry, and an encrypted copy of the document.
  • Health: sickness absence, its reason, and fit notes (encrypted). Your employer must have a specific legal condition and a policy document to hold these.
  • Messages: work messages with your managers, read receipts, and your choice of WhatsApp, text or email with your consent.
  • Wellbeing: weekly pulse answers are stored without your name and shown only when at least five people at a site have answered.
  • Pay figures passed to your employer's payroll provider: name, date of birth, pay basis, hours, premiums and tips. Preshift does not hold bank details.
  • Scores: some employers use absence scores (Bradford factor), shift fairness counts, lateness and no-show counts, and a retention risk indicator. These are information for managers. Ask your employer how it uses them; you may ask for any decision about you to be reviewed by a person.

AI. If your employer uses Preshift's AI assistant, your name, roles, pay rate, age, availability, leave, hours, and lateness and no-show counts may be sent to Anthropic (the provider of Claude, based in the US) to draft a rota, under your employer's own agreement with Anthropic. A manager reviews every draft before it is used. Your NI number, address, absence reasons, HR notes and bank or medical details are not sent.

Your rights are exercised with your employer. You can see and download your own data in the staff app. If you contact us, we will pass your request to your employer promptly.
