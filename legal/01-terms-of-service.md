## 4. What the Service is not: your responsibilities as employer

This clause matters more than any other. Read it.

4.1 **You remain the employer.** You are solely responsible for your employment decisions and
for complying with employment, working time, national minimum wage, holiday pay, right to work,
equality, health and safety, tax and data protection law. The Service is a tool that helps you

4.2 **Compliance checks are aids, not guarantees.** The Service checks rotas and records against
the rules we have built in (for example working time limits, rest breaks, minimum wage rates and
young worker rules). Those checks depend on the data and settings you give it, cannot cover every
contract term, collective agreement or individual circumstance, and may lag a change in the law.
A rota that passes every check is not thereby lawful, and a warning not shown is not a
confirmation that no breach exists.

4.3 **Figures are estimates until payroll runs.** Shift costs, loaded labour, holiday accrual,
tronc, SSP, labour cash forecasts, journals and payroll exports are calculated from your data and
our models. Your payroll provider or bureau remains responsible for calculating and paying wages,
deductions, PAYE, National Insurance, pensions and tronc, and for filings to HMRC. Where we also
run your payroll through our separate TeamPay payroll service, that service is governed by its
own agreement and data processing terms, not by these Terms, and Outputs from Preshift Rota
remain estimates until the payroll run is approved under that agreement. You must check
Outputs before relying on them, posting them or paying from them.

4.4 **No professional advice.** Nothing in the Service, its help pages or its AI features is
legal, employment, tax, payroll or accounting advice. If you are a client of Williams, Stanley &
Co., any professional advice it gives you is under your separate engagement letter with it, not
under these Terms.

4.5 **AI features.** Some features use artificial intelligence to draft rotas, answer questions,
summarise or suggest actions. AI Outputs can be wrong or incomplete. The Service presents every
AI change as a proposal for a person to confirm, and that person must review it before applying
it. You must not use the Service to make a decision that has a legal or similarly significant
effect on a member of staff (for example dismissal, discipline, pay or refusal of work) based
solely on automated processing, without meaningful human review.

4.6 **Automatic settings you choose.** Some settings act without a manager in the loop, for
example approving an open shift claim automatically when it is within the rules and the labour
budget, giving a shift to the first eligible person to accept, offering shifts cheapest first, or
scoring absence (Bradford factor) and retention risk. These are off unless you switch them on, or
are information for a manager rather than a decision. Where you switch them on, you decide to use
them as employer, you must tell affected staff how they work, and you must give staff a way to
ask a manager to review an outcome.

4.7 **Your own AI account.** AI features run on the Claude API account and key you save in
Settings, under your own agreement with Anthropic, and Anthropic's charges are billed to you by
Anthropic. Data sent to Anthropic through your key is sent on your instructions and governed by

numbers, addresses, absence reasons, HR notes, bank or medical details, but it will pass on
anything a manager types into the assistant, so managers must not type special category data

## Schedule: our pricing approach

Adapted from the WS fixed price statement.

We price per site, never per head. Hospitality hires seasonally and turns staff over fast, and a
per head price taxes exactly the flexibility a rota exists to manage. You know what you will pay
before you start, the price falls per site as you grow, and nobody's bill jumps at a threshold.

If you are not satisfied with the Service, tell us straight away. We would rather have the chance
to put it right and win back your trust. If we cannot, we will work with you towards a fair
outcome on what you have paid for the period concerned.
