# Preshift Rota: Staff Terms of Use

DRAFT v0.1, 1 November 2026. Shown to a member of staff the first time they sign in to the staff
app or kiosk. Plain English on purpose: the reader is a bartender on a phone, not a lawyer. Keep
it to one screen.

---

**Your employer uses Preshift Rota to run rotas, clocking in, leave and messages at work.**

1. **Who is responsible.** Your employer gave you this login and decides how Preshift is used.
   Your employer is responsible for your personal data in Preshift. We, TeamPay Payroll Limited (trading as Preshift Rota),
   run the software for them. Your employer's privacy notice explains how they use your data;
   ask your manager for it, or read the summary at [preshift.co.uk/privacy#staff].

2. **Your login is yours.** Do not share your password, PIN or authenticator, and do not clock
   in or out for anyone else. Clocking for someone else may be treated by your employer as a
   disciplinary matter.

3. **Clocking in and your location.** Preshift records when you clock in and out, and how (card,
   kiosk PIN, phone tag or the app). If your employer uses location clocking, your phone's
   position is checked and saved only at the moment you clock in or out, to confirm you are at
   work, and is deleted after [3 months]. Preshift never tracks you at any other time.

4. **Messages.** You choose whether work messages reach you by WhatsApp, text or email, and you
   can change that in Settings at any time. Your employer will not use them for marketing.

5. **Your rights.** You can see your own data in the app, download a copy, and ask for it to be
   corrected. To make any other request (for example to object or to have data deleted), ask
   your employer. If you contact us, we will pass your request to them.

6. **Use it for work.** Do not misuse the app, try to see other people's data, or upload anything
   offensive or unlawful.

7. **No charge.** Preshift is free for you. Your employer pays for it.

8. **When you leave.** Your access ends when your employer removes you. Download anything you
   want to keep (for example payslip summaries or holiday records) before your last day.
