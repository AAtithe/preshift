# Preshift Rota: Data Processing Agreement

DRAFT v0.1, 1 November 2026. Forms part of the Terms of Service and applies automatically to
every customer. Publish at [preshift.co.uk/dpa]. Meets UK GDPR Article 28(3).

Note on source: the WS Standard Terms of Business say only "The parties shall comply with their
data protection obligations as set out in Williams Stanley & Co's Data Protection Policy" (clause
7). A policy is not a contract and does not satisfy Article 28. This DPA should not be built on
that clause, and WS's own terms need the same fix (to-do list W1).

---

## 1. Parties and roles

1.1 This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is between the Customer (controller) and TeamPay
Payroll Limited (company number 17197586, trading as Preshift Rota) (processor), and forms part of the Agreement defined in the Terms of Service. Terms
defined there have the same meaning here.

1.2 Where a Customer group has several employing companies, the Customer enters into this DPA for
itself and as agent for each employing company in its organisation, each of which is a
controller of its own staff's data.

1.3 "Data Protection Law" means the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and
Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, each as
amended. "Personal Data", "Special Category Data", "Data Subject", "Processing", "Controller",
"Processor" and "Personal Data Breach" have the meanings in Data Protection Law.

## 2. Processing

2.1 We will process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions,
including as to international transfers, unless the law requires otherwise (in which case we will
tell the Customer first, unless the law prohibits it). The Agreement, the Customer's
configuration of the Service, and its users' actions in the Service are the Customer's complete
instructions. We will tell the Customer immediately if we think an instruction breaks Data
Protection Law.

2.2 The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing, the types of Personal Data
and categories of Data Subject are in Annex 1.

2.3 The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis, and where needed a condition under
Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018 and an appropriate policy document, for all Personal
Data it puts into the Service, and for giving Data Subjects the information Articles 13 and 14
require.

## 3. Our people

We will ensure everyone we authorise to process Customer Personal Data is bound by
confidentiality, is trained in data protection, and has access only to what their role needs.
Access by our staff to Customer Personal Data for support is logged.

## 4. Security

We will implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures in Annex 2, appropriate
to the risk, as Article 32 requires. We may update them provided the overall level of protection
is not reduced.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Customer gives general authorisation for us to use the sub-processors listed at
[preshift.co.uk/subprocessors] (Annex 3).

5.2 We will give at least [30] days' notice of any new or replacement sub-processor by email to
the account owner and on the list page. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection
grounds within that period. If we cannot reasonably accommodate the objection, the Customer may
terminate the affected part of the Service and receive a pro rata refund of prepaid Fees.

5.3 We will impose data protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective
than this DPA, and remain liable to the Customer for each sub-processor's performance.

5.4 Providers the Customer contracts with directly and connects using its own account or key
(including its own Claude API account, email sending account, WhatsApp Business account, Twilio
account and EPOS) are the Customer's processors, not our sub-processors.

5.5 Where the Customer also uses our TeamPay payroll service, we process payroll data under that
service's own data processing terms. Data passed from Preshift Rota to the TeamPay payroll service
is passed on the Customer's instruction and used only for running the Customer's payroll; we keep
the two services' purposes, access and records separate.

## 6. International transfers

We will not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the UK except to a country covered by UK
adequacy regulations, or under the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to
the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, supported by a transfer risk assessment. The mechanism for
each sub-processor is in Annex 3.

## 7. Assisting the Customer

7.1 **Data Subject requests.** The Service provides tools for the Customer to respond to requests
under Articles 15 to 21 (export, correction, erasure, restriction, and a request log). If we
receive a request directly, we will pass it to the Customer within [3] Business Days and not
respond ourselves except to acknowledge it.

7.2 **Other assistance.** Taking into account the nature of processing and information available
to us, we will provide reasonable assistance with the Customer's obligations under Articles 32 to
36 (security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation).
We may charge at our then current rates for assistance beyond providing standard documentation.

## 8. Personal Data Breaches

8.1 We will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within [48] hours, after
becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

8.2 The notice will describe, as far as then known: the nature of the breach, the categories and
approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, the measures
taken or proposed, and a contact point. We will supplement it as more becomes known.

8.3 We will take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach, and will not inform third
parties (other than law enforcement or a regulator where required) without the Customer's
consent. The Customer is responsible for notifying the ICO within 72 hours and Data Subjects where
required.

## 9. Deletion and return

9.1 On termination, the Service stays available read-only for [30] days so the Customer can
export Customer Personal Data. We then delete it within [60] days, and backups are overwritten
within [35] days after that, unless the law requires us to keep it.

9.2 During the Agreement, the Service applies the retention periods the Customer sets in
Privacy. [Product gap: retention is applied when an administrator presses Apply, not
automatically. To-do list P4.]

## 10. Audit

10.1 We will make available all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with
Article 28, including this DPA, Annex 2, the sub-processor list, and [our Cyber Essentials Plus
certificate and the summary of our latest independent penetration test].

10.2 Where that information is not reasonably sufficient, or a regulator requires it, the
Customer (or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality) may audit our compliance on at
least [30] days' notice, no more than once in any 12 months, during business hours, at the
Customer's cost, and without access to other customers' data.

## 11. Liability and precedence

11.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in clause 13 of the Terms of
Service.

11.2 If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of Service on the processing of Personal Data, this DPA
prevails.

## Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or above) and at rest (database provider encryption).
- Application level AES-256-GCM encryption of right to work documents, fit notes and HR files,
  with the key held outside the database; every view logged.
- Passwords hashed with scrypt; authenticator secrets encrypted; kiosk PINs hashed; invitation
  and reset tokens stored only as hashes.
- Two-factor authentication required for pay, payroll, journal, settings and access roles.
- Role and site based access control, enforced on the server for every request.
- Audit log of changes and of document views.
- Tenant isolation: each organisation's data scoped by organisation on every query; automated
  tests covering access boundaries.
- Rate limiting on sign-in, sign-up and demo.
- Data minimisation in AI features: NI numbers, addresses, absence reasons, HR notes, bank and
  medical details excluded from data sent to AI models.
- Backups by the database provider with point in time recovery [confirm retention period].
- Hosting and database region: United Kingdom (London). Customer Data at rest is stored only in
  the UK.
- Staff confidentiality obligations, data protection training on joining and annually.
- Incident response procedure with breach notification under clause 8 [to write. To-do list D6].
- [Cyber Essentials Plus certification and annual independent penetration test. To-do list S1,
  S2.]
