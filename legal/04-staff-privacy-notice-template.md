# Template: privacy notice insert for customers' staff

DRAFT v0.1, 1 November 2026. Customers are controllers of their staff's data and must tell staff
how it is used (UK GDPR Article 13). Most hospitality employers' notices predate tools like this.
We give them this insert to add to their own employee privacy notice, from Help in the app and
in onboarding. It is a template for the employer to adapt, not advice; the help page must say so.

---

## [EMPLOYER NAME]: how we use Preshift Rota

We use Preshift Rota, provided by TeamPay Payroll Limited, to plan rotas, record when you work, manage
leave and absence, keep HR records, message you about work, and work out labour costs. Preshift
acts on our instructions. We remain responsible for your data.

**What we keep in Preshift and why**

| Information | Why | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, date of birth, contact details, roles, pay, hours, contract details | Run your employment, pay you correctly, apply young worker and minimum wage rules | Contract; legal obligation |
| Availability, leave, shifts, clock-in and out times and method | Plan and record work; working time and holiday records | Contract; legal obligation |
| [Location at the moment you clock in or out, kept [3 months]] | Confirm clocking happens at work | Legitimate interests |
| Right to work documents and checks | Required by immigration law | Legal obligation |
| Sickness absence, reasons and fit notes | Manage absence and pay SSP; support you at work | Legal obligation; employment law condition (DPA 2018 Sch 1 para 1), under our appropriate policy document |
| NI number, address, emergency contact | Payroll and your safety | Contract; legal obligation; vital interests |
| HR records: training, reviews, formal cases | Manage performance and conduct fairly | Contract; legitimate interests |
| Messages and your channel choice | Keep you informed about work | Legitimate interests; consent for WhatsApp and text |
| [Absence (Bradford) scores, lateness and no-show counts, shift fairness, retention risk] | Help managers spot problems early and share hard shifts fairly | Legitimate interests |

**Automated features.** [Delete what we do not use.] Open shifts may be offered to eligible
people cheapest first, and a claim may be approved automatically when it meets the rules and the
labour budget. An AI tool may draft rotas using your availability, hours, pay rate and lateness and
no-show counts; a manager reviews every draft. Scores are information for managers, not decisions.
You can ask a manager to review any outcome that affects you.

**Who else sees it.** Our payroll provider [NAME] receives your pay details. [AI drafting uses
Anthropic (Claude), based in the US.] Preshift's own providers are listed at
[preshift.co.uk/subprocessors].

**How long.** [Location 3 months. Contact details 3 months after you leave. Right to work 2 years
after you leave. Pay records 6 years after the tax year. Messages 13 months.]

**Your rights.** You can see and download your data in the staff app. For anything else, ask
[NAME / EMAIL]. You can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.
